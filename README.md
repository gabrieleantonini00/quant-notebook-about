# Quant Notebook

[Visit the website](https://gabrieleantonini00.github.io/quant-notebook/)

I'm Gabriele Antonini, a PhD candidate in Econometrics. I created Quant Notebook to collect the notes I have taken throughout my studies and share them with other students and anyone interested in quantitative methods.

The notes cover econometrics, probability and statistics, statistical learning, natural language processing, and quantitative finance. My aim is to bring together intuition, mathematical derivations, and worked examples in an accessible resource for learning and revision.
